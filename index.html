How these numbers are worked out
  • Holdings come from each fund's 13F-HR information table on SEC EDGAR for 30 Jun and 30 Sep 2026.
  • Prices are reported value divided by shares, median across filers at each quarter end. Splits in the quarter (e.g. CrowdStrike 4-for-1, Amphenol 2-for-1) are adjusted; CUSIP changes (e.g. Exxon Mobil to ExxonMobil Holdings) are joined.
  • Norges Bank's Q3 filing has a one-line placeholder table, so it counts as filed but is excluded from holdings figures.
  • 13F filings show long US-listed equities and options only, as at quarter end, filed up to 45 days later. Not investment advice.
